I am a condensed matter theorist working on correlated lattice systems, exciton–phonon coupling, and non-Hermitian quantum dynamics. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from UC Riverside, advised by Prof. Vivek Aji.

Here I share numerical models and research code, with calculation examples, figures, and numerical checks.

[Website](https://lyfstefan.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mXwRRVQAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1453-9851)

## <img src="assets/research-heading.svg#gh-light-mode-only" alt="Research" width="122" height="34"><img src="assets/research-heading-dark.svg#gh-dark-mode-only" alt="Research" width="122" height="34">

- **Correlated phases** — how interactions select bond ordering and loop-current patterns in lattice models.
- **Exciton–phonon coupling** — how phonons shape absorption spectra and their temperature dependence in TMD heterostructures.
- **Non-Hermitian dynamics** — how gain, loss, and phase defects control localization in quantum walks.

More about my [research](https://lyfstefan.github.io/research/) and [publications](https://lyfstefan.github.io/publications/).

| Project | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| [phonon-assisted-absorption](https://github.com/lyfstefan/phonon-assisted-absorption) | Computes exciton–phonon absorption spectra in TMD heterostructures, with temperature dependence, channel decomposition, and synthetic-spectrum fitting. Accompanies [PRB **113**, 235306 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/v3sr-lg47). |
| [bond-order-flux-phases](https://github.com/lyfstefan/bond-order-flux-phases) | Optimizes bond-current patterns in an interacting square-lattice model; compares flux subspaces and maps currents, bond anisotropy, and flux. Accompanies [PRB **113**, 045140 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/3jqj-28q7). |
| [nonhermitian-quantum-walks](https://github.com/lyfstefan/nonhermitian-quantum-walks) | Reimplements phase-defect localization and time evolution in two non-Hermitian quantum walks, with numerical comparisons to Appendix D of [PRA **102**, 022218 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.102.022218). |
| [1988_Affleck](https://github.com/lyfstefan/1988_Affleck) | Explores a separate mean-field model with four complex bond parameters, comparing uniform, flux, kite, Peierls, and stripe-like configurations across filling and hopping strength. |
